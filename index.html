PhD Researcher · VITA Lab · EPFLAdvised by Prof. Alexandre Alahi

My PhD research is on 3D human motion. I started with 3D pose estimation, moved to multimodal pose forecasting (CVPR 2025), and now work on physics-informed generation and evaluation of human–human interaction (RA-L 2025), using physics simulation and reinforcement learning. Before my PhD, I worked on humanoid robotics, training reinforcement-learning locomotion controllers for the Surena IV humanoid.

I am open to both research internships and full-time Research Scientist / Research Engineer roles in computer vision, machine learning, and robotics. I expect to complete my PhD in 2027, and the exact month is flexible depending on the position.
