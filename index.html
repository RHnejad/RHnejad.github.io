About

I'm a PhD researcher in robotics at EPFL (EDRS), advised by Prof. Alexandre Alahi at the VITA Lab. I use computer vision, machine learning, and robotics to study 3D human motion, including pose estimation, forecasting, and physics-informed generation (CVPR 2025, RA-L 2025).

My background is in humanoid robotics, control, and manipulation. I have also supervised student projects on 3D car pose estimation and lane detection.
