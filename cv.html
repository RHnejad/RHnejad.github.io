Experience

2022 — Present

Doctoral Assistant

VITA Lab, EPFL · Switzerland

  • Simulating human–human interactions in Newton (NVIDIA Warp) for physics-informed motion generation, and using reinforcement learning in simulation (ProtoMotions with Newton; PHC with Isaac Gym) to make the generated motion physically feasible.
  • Developing metrics that use physics simulation to judge whether generated human–human motion is plausible (paper in preparation).
  • Co-first author of HHI-Assist (RA-L 2025, presented at ICRA): a motion-capture dataset and benchmark of close-contact assistance between people, with a Transformer-based diffusion baseline. Now extending it to robot-assistance experiments in simulation.
  • Co-first author of MotionMap (CVPR 2025): a heatmap representation that captures several plausible futures and their uncertainty, reaching state-of-the-art results in human motion forecasting.
  • Used cross-task consistency between direct 3D estimation and 2D→3D lifting to improve 3D human pose estimation.
  • Used TrajNet++ to forecast the movement of professional hockey players from positional data.
  • Built a pipeline to create a large Persian speech dataset and fine-tuned an LLM-based text-to-speech model on it with multi-GPU training, using both full fine-tuning and LoRA.
  • Building a driving world model by conditioning the LTX-2 video generation model on embeddings from a Newton-based driving simulator.
  • Working with the NeuroRestore lab on a virtual-patient model that maps spinal cord stimulation to muscle (EMG) responses, using MyoSuite on MuJoCo.
